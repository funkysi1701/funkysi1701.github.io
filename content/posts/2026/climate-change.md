## Intro

Apologies this post is a bit off topic but hopefully still interesting. It has been a hot summer, with quite a few heat waves with temperatures reaching over 30 degrees. I am not a environmental person but recent weather is increasingly making me think we need to take better care of our planet.

There is a film called the National Emergency Briefing that is being screened in churches, and communities that talks about the issues far better than I can. Check out [National Emergency Briefing](https://www.nebriefing.org/) for where you can find a screening near you.

But what can we do? [Our Health Our Planet](https://ourhealthourplanet.co.uk/public-action-impact) suggests a few areas we can concentrate our efforts on.

## Bank

The first item on the list is switching to a bank that does not invest in fossil fuels. I bank with [Monzo](/tools-and-resources/#monzo-bank) so lets look at how they do.

- Monzo says it doesn't invest customer deposits in fossil-fuel-based energy companies, arms companies or tobacco companies.
- Its offices use 100% renewable electricity.
- 93% of cards issued in FY2025 were made using recycled PVC, with solvent-free ink.
- Monzo has committed to a net-zero target for 2030 and publishes its carbon footprint annually.

- A 28% increase in total emissions between FY2025 and FY2026. Most come from its supply chain, rather than its offices.
- Its net-zero progress is behind target.
- It doesn't actively finance green projects.

## Energy

I use [Octopus Energy](/tools-and-resources/#octopus-energy) for my energy, lets look at them next.

One of Octopus's biggest strength is renewable electricity

- It supplies green electricity tariffs backed by renewable generation.
- It has substantial involvement in wind and solar generation.
- It invests in renewable energy projects through its generation business.
- It supports flexibility in the electricity grid, helping accommodate intermittent renewable generation.

In June 2026, Octopus Energy Generation announced:

- £8.5 billion in renewable energy assets under management.
- 5.7 GW of renewable energy projects.
- More than 400 sites across 21 countries.
- £930 million deployed into clean energy projects in just six months.

Octopus is still a major gas supplier. Its 2026 environmental reporting shows that gas accounts for approximately 92% of its overall corporate carbon footprint.

## MP

Our Health Our Planet says we need to speak out and talk to our MP about the need for climate action. Lets look at my MP here, but to find out more about how your MP votes on green issues look at [Vote Climate](https://voteclimate.uk)

### Examples of votes supporting environmental action

| Date | Parliamentary vote |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Sep 2024 | Great British Energy Bill |
| Oct 2024 | Great British Energy Bill, third reading |
| Jan 2025 | Clean Heat Market Mechanism |
| Apr 2025 | Onshore wind and solar planning |
| Feb 2026 | Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme |
| Jun 2026 | Seventh Carbon Budget |

Vote Climate gives my MP a score of 16 / 21 in favour of Climate issues.

Of course there is always more they can do and writing a letter or sending them a message about our climate concerns is a great thing to do.
